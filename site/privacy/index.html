Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

  • These are personal projects run by one person, Raufi Musaddiq, not a company.
  • I collect only what each service needs to work. I don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Sign in with Google gives me your name, email address and profile picture. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • Email raufi@musaddiqr.com and I'll export or delete your data.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to musaddiqr.com and to the services I run on its subdomains:

finance.musaddiqr.com

Finance

Household finance: transactions, budgets, documents and reviews for a family workspace.

Own policy: finance.musaddiqr.com/privacy

ai.musaddiqr.com

LiteRouter

An AI routing gateway that forwards requests from access-key holders to AI model providers.

idx.musaddiqr.com

IDX data provider

A read-only Indonesian market data API with a developer portal and API keys.

antares.musaddiqr.com

Antares

A personal AI chat assistant for approved users.

Finance also has its own privacy policy, in Indonesian, at finance.musaddiqr.com/privacy. For Finance, that policy applies first, and this page covers anything it doesn't.

The data controller for all of them is Raufi Musaddiq, based in Indonesia. Contact: raufi@musaddiqr.com.

musaddiqr.com itself is a static portfolio. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no sign-in.

2. What I collect

Account data

Your name, email address and profile picture, plus a stable account identifier from your sign-in provider. I also keep your role, whether your account is approved, and when you last signed in.

Content you provide

  • Finance: transactions, categories, budgets and notes you enter. Also receipts and documents you upload, bank notification emails you forward to the service, and the Telegram connection if you turn it on.
  • Antares: your conversations, prompts, files you attach, saved templates and settings.
  • IDX data provider: the API keys you create and their names.
  • LiteRouter: provider connections and access keys that the operator configures.

Usage and technical data

Request times, token counts, the model used, estimated cost, IP address, browser type, and error logs. I use these to enforce rate limits and quotas, to keep the services secure, and to fix problems.

Cookies

Only strictly necessary cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a short-lived security cookie during sign-in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Google user data

Finance, Antares and the IDX data provider let you sign in with Google. When you do, I request only these scopes: openid, email and profile. Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier.

  • How I use it: to create and identify your account, to show your name and picture in the app, and to contact you about your account.
  • What I don't do: I don't access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data. I don't use Google user data for advertising. I don't sell it. I don't use it to train AI models.
  • Sharing: I don't share Google user data with anyone, except as section 5 describes.
  • Storage and deletion: it is stored with your account and deleted when your account is deleted.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How I use data

  • To provide each service: show your data, run your requests, and keep your workspace separate from others.
  • To secure the services: authentication, abuse prevention, rate limits and audit logs.
  • To keep the services working and improve them, based on errors and aggregate usage.
  • To contact you about your account or about changes to these documents.

Where a service uses AI, your input is sent to an AI model to produce the result you asked for. In Finance, for example, a model reads a forwarded bank email so the transaction can be recorded. I don't use your content to train models.

5. Who I share data with

I don't sell or rent personal data. I share it only:

  • With service providers that run the infrastructure: Cloudflare (DNS, network and security), the server host, and Google for sign-in.
  • With AI model providers: the text and files you send to an AI feature go to the model provider that answers them, under that provider's terms.
  • Inside a workspace you share: for example, members of the same Finance family workspace see that workspace's data.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of users or of me.

6. Storage, security and retention

Data is stored on servers I manage, behind Cloudflare. Connections use HTTPS. Access is restricted to authenticated users and to me as the operator. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect your data.

I keep your data while your account is active. When you delete content or ask me to delete your account, I delete it from the live system within 30 days. Backups roll over and are cleared within a further 30 days. Logs are kept only as long as they're needed for security and debugging.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See, correct or export the data I hold about you.
  • Delete individual items in the app, or ask me to delete your whole account.
  • Withdraw consent, or object to a particular use.
  • Revoke this site's access to your Google account at any time, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

To use any of these rights, email raufi@musaddiqr.com from the address on your account. I'll reply within 30 days. These rights apply under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) and similar laws where you live.

8. Children

The services are not intended for anyone under 18, and I don't knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given me data, tell me and I'll delete it.

9. International transfers

Infrastructure and AI providers may process data outside Indonesia. When that happens, I rely on those providers' safeguards for the transfer.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, I'll also notify signed-in users in the app or by email before it takes effect.
